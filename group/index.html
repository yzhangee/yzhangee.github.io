Knowledge-integrated Machine Intelligence Lab

Understanding the world through knowledge.

We integrate data-driven learning with knowledge to build AI systems that understand, reason, create, and act. Our goal is to make these systems reliable, efficient, and useful for scientific discovery and real-world problem solving.

Themes

Research Directions

Multimodal & Agentic Intelligence

  • Multimodal Learning
  • Retrieval & RAG
  • Generation
  • Agentic Reasoning

Acquiring and organizing knowledge from multimodal data to support understanding, generation, and agentic reasoning, enabling models to retrieve evidence, plan, and act.

Efficient LLM Architectures & Systems

  • Architectures
  • Training
  • Inference
  • Long Context

Designing LLM architectures and systems that learn, retain, and use knowledge efficiently, reducing the computational and memory costs of training and inference.

AI for Science & Engineering

  • Biomedicine
  • Materials
  • Engineering

Integrating domain knowledge with data-driven learning to develop models and intelligent systems for biomedicine, materials, and engineering, supporting scientific discovery and practical problem solving.
